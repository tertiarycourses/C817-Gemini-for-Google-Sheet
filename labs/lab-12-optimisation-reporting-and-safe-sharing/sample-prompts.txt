Gemini for Google Sheet (C817) — Lab 12: Optimise the Hiring Decision and Share Results Safely
Sample prompts for Gemini in Google Sheets. Copy one at a time, adapt it, and verify every result.
Prompts web page (one-click Copy): https://tertiarycourses.github.io/C817-Gemini-for-Google-Sheet/prompts/#lab-12

[1] Optimise
What is the highest total Weighted_Score possible if the budget is reduced by S$2,000?

[2] Optimise
Schedule the eight final interviews across Monday and Tuesday so no interviewer has more than three in a row.

[3] Docs
Turn the recommendation into a one-page memo with a table of hires and start dates.

[4] Gmail
Catch me up on the latest emails about the Q3 hiring drive.

[5] Share
Which tabs in this spreadsheet would be risky to share outside Orchid Bay, and why?

------------------------------------------------------------
Step prompts used in this lab
------------------------------------------------------------

[Step 1]
Required state:
File      : OrchidBay_Hiring_Decision (Google Sheets copy)
Tabs      : About_This_Data | Finalists (16 rows) | Constraints | Prompt_Log
Budget    : Constraints!B8 = S$41,900 per month for 8 hires

[Step 2]
ASK GEMINI:
I need to choose hires from the Finalists tab. Maximise the total Weighted_Score. Hire exactly the number of Openings for each Position on the Constraints tab, and keep the total Expected_Salary of everyone hired at or below the budget in Constraints!B8. Which candidates should I hire?

[Step 3]
ASK GEMINI:
If there were no budget limit, which candidates would you pick, what would they cost, and what is the trade-off compared with your answer?

[Step 4]
ACTION:
Add a checkbox column called Selected at the end of the table on the Finalists tab, unchecked by default.

CHECK:
Total salary     =SUMIFS(Finalists!F2:F17,Finalists!M2:M17,TRUE)
Total score      =SUMIFS(Finalists!K2:K17,Finalists!M2:M17,TRUE)
Within budget    =SUMIFS(Finalists!F2:F17,Finalists!M2:M17,TRUE)<=Constraints!B8
Data Analysts    =COUNTIFS(Finalists!C2:C17,"Data Analyst",Finalists!M2:M17,TRUE)
All hires        =COUNTIF(Finalists!M2:M17,TRUE)

[Step 5]
ASK GEMINI:
Write a hiring recommendation for the client in under 200 words: the eight recommended hires by role, the total salary against the budget, the one trade-off made because of the budget, and the risks to check (notice periods and any must-have gaps).

ASK GEMINI:
What are the main points of OrchidBay_Screening_Rubric? Does my recommendation follow its fair screening rules?

[Step 6]
ASK GEMINI:
Before I share this spreadsheet with the client, which columns contain personal data they don't need to see?

ACTION:
Create a new tab called Share_View with only Candidate_ID, Position, Weighted_Score and Selected for the rows where Selected is checked.

[Step 7]
LOG:
List the prompts I sent you in this conversation. For each one, give a one-line summary of what you did, so I can copy it into the Prompt_Log tab.

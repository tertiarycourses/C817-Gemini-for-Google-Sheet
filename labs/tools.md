# Tools & Accounts — Gemini for Google Sheet (C817)

**Course Code:** C817  |  **Version v1.0 · 27 July 2026**

Everything in this course runs in the browser. You need no installs beyond a modern browser.

## Accounts

- **Google account** with access to **Google Sheets** and **Google Drive**.
- **Gemini in Google Sheets** enabled on that account (the trainer confirms access at the start of the day).

## Apps used

- **Google Sheets** — where all the work happens: formulas, cleaning, pivots and charts.
- **Gemini side panel ('Ask Gemini')** — the AI assistant inside Sheets for prompts, formulas and analysis.
- **The =AI() function** — an optional way to return Gemini output directly into a cell (used where a lab notes it).
- **Google Drive** — where your own copy of the sample sheet is saved.

## Sample data

- **'Riverside Supplies — Q1 Orders'** Google Sheet — the trainer shares a link; make your own copy with **File > Make a copy**. Columns: Order ID, Order Date, Customer, Region, Product, Category, Quantity, Unit Price, Total.

## Safe use

- Use only data you are authorised to use. Never paste passwords, personal identifiers or confidential business data into an AI prompt — use the sample data if in doubt.
- Verify every AI figure or formula against a value you can confirm before you rely on it.

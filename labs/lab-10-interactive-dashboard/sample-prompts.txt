Gemini for Google Sheet (C817) — Lab 10: Make the Hiring Dashboard Interactive and Visual
Sample prompts for Gemini in Google Sheets. Copy one at a time, adapt it, and verify every result.
Prompts web page (one-click Copy): https://tertiarycourses.github.io/C817-Gemini-for-Google-Sheet/prompts/#lab-10

[1] Image
Create an image of a simple header banner with an orchid and the words Hiring Dashboard.

[2] Layout
Suggest a clean layout for this dashboard: where should the KPIs, charts and selectors go?

[3] Alert
Highlight the Hires cells in the monthly table that are 0 in light grey.

[4] Number format
Format F7 as a percentage with one decimal place and G7:H7 with one decimal place.

[5] Review
Review this dashboard for a colleague: what is unclear, and what would you add?
